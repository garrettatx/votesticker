# Classic National Stickers on the Home Page (Spec, Not Approved)

Written October 26, 2026, from Garrett's question with four reference images (a classic red, white and
blue set; Philadelphia sticker designs; Rhode Island contest entries). Waiting for Garrett's outside
feedback before any mockups.

## Why

The biggest queries are national: "i voted early sticker" (184 impressions a week, spread over five
state pages), "i voted stickers by state" (home page, position 7.1), and "i voted sticker" in general.
State pages win "[state] i voted sticker"; nothing serves someone who wants a classic sticker without
picking a state. A home page section strengthens the page Google already ranks for these queries; a
separate page would compete with it.

## What

A "Classic I Voted Stickers" section on the home page: 8 to 12 national stickers, each a one-tap
download, drawn by our renderer (new pieces: a stripe field, star rows, a star ring; existing: sunburst,
check, banner and type). Spanish home page: the same set with YA VOTÉ, MI PRIMER VOTO, YO TAMBIÉN
VOTARÉ.

**Messages:** I VOTED, I VOTED TODAY, I VOTED EARLY, I VOTED BY MAIL, FIRST-TIME VOTER, FUTURE VOTER.
Candidates for Garrett: I WORKED THE POLLS, WE VOTED, VOTED WITH MY KIDS.

**Order on the page:** hero and state finder (unchanged) > Classic stickers > Find a Sticker You Like >
I Voted Early Stickers > How It Works. The section ends: "Want one with your state? Pick your state
above."

## Rules

- Conventions only (stars, stripes, rays, rings, ribbons, checkmarks, a ballot-box drop). No copying or
  close imitation of any specific design in the references: the Rhode Island and Philadelphia pieces
  belong to their artists and offices.
- No state, county or government marks; nothing that reads as an official sticker.
- Identity-group stickers (LGBTQ Voter, Veteran Voter and others): not in this round. Choosing which
  groups to include invites "why them and not us"; civic roles and life stages carry the same personal
  feel without that. Garrett's call if revisited, and then as a broad set offered equally.
- Descriptive file names (`i-voted-today-stars-and-stripes-sticker.jpg`), alt text, visible captions,
  image sitemap entries, direct download; `ImageObject` after reuse terms are decided.
- Render first: a sheet of 12 at full size and 40 px for Garrett to pick from, then build.

## Measure

GA4 `sticker_save` with `sticker_source: classic`; Search Console home page queries "i voted sticker",
"i voted early sticker", "first time voter sticker", "future voter sticker", image search impressions.
