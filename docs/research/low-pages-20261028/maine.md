Fixing ballot-envelope problems

Your missing-signature FAQ is too categorical:

“If you didn't, a missing signature means your ballot is rejected.”

The statute distinguishes notification obligations from cure procedures. Missing signatures can be cured in person; telephone confirmation can result in an accepted-but-challenged ballot. A mismatched signature has different treatment and does not automatically mean rejection.
