When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in West Virginia runs Wednesday, October 21 to Saturday, October 31. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Vote early at the county courthouse, an annex or a community voting location during business hours, and 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on the last two Saturdays.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. at your assigned polling place.
ID: Yes. Show an unexpired photo ID, such as a West Virginia driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a West Virginia student ID. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your county courthouse, an annex or a community voting site October 21 to 31. On Election Day, you must vote at your assigned polling place.
Look up your Election Day polling place with the state's search. Early voting sites and hours for all 55 counties are on the Secretary of State's early voting locations page.
Voting for the first time in West Virginia or your county? Bring a valid ID with your current name and address: a photo ID, a utility bill, a bank statement, a government check, a paycheck or another government document. Voting absentee? Put a copy in the return envelope, outside the secrecy envelope.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
West Virginia driver's license or state-issued ID
Driver's license from another state
U.S. passport or passport card
Government employee ID with photo, from a local, state or federal government
Student ID with photo from a West Virginia college or high school
U.S. military ID with photo
Voter registration card with photo from a West Virginia county clerk or the Secretary of State
DMV ID without a photo, for voters whose recognized religious community forbids being photographed
Your ID can't be expired. If you're 65 or older, an expired ID still works as long as it hadn't expired on your 65th birthday.
No ID? An adult who has known you for at least six months can sign an affidavit and show their own photo ID, or a poll worker who has known you that long can vouch for you. You can also get a free voter ID card at your county clerk's office.

Who Can Vote by Mail
You can vote absentee by mail only if you can't vote in person during all of early voting and on Election Day, for a reason the law allows, such as:
illness, injury or another medical reason
a disability or advanced age that keeps you from getting around
incarceration or home detention, unless you're serving a sentence for a felony, treason or election bribery
your work hours and distance from the county seat
an early voting site and polling place you can't get into
personal or business travel
attending college or another school or training
living outside the county for now
serving as an elected or appointed state or federal official

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign gear at home. Within 100 feet of the outside entrance of the building where you vote, no one can display signs or other campaign items, hand out literature or ask for votes. The same goes for early voting sites while voting is going on. A bumper sticker on your parked car is fine.
If you're in line when the polls close, stay in line. You can still vote.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official West Virginia sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in West Virginia on Election Day?
Polls are open 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. at your assigned polling place.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in West Virginia?
Yes. Show an unexpired photo ID, such as a West Virginia driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a West Virginia student ID. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in West Virginia?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 13, 2026, and West Virginia has no Election Day registration.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in West Virginia?
Not in the voting booth. West Virginia law bars taking a recording device into the booth to record voting.
From the West Virginia Code .
When is the mail ballot deadline in West Virginia?
By mail: postmarked by Tuesday, November 3, and received by your county clerk by the start of canvass, Monday, November 9. By hand: at your county clerk's office by Monday, November 2. No one can hand-deliver more than two voters' ballots.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in West Virginia, and does it count?
If poll workers can't find your name in the poll book, or there's another question about whether you can vote, they must offer you a provisional ballot. It counts if the Board of Canvassers finds you're eligible and registered.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in West Virginia?
You can still vote. Anyone in line at the polling place when the polls close gets to vote, even if the line runs outside.
From the West Virginia Code .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in West Virginia?
Look up your absentee ballot's status with the Secretary of State's absentee ballot tracker.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in West Virginia?
A mailed absentee ballot still counts if it's postmarked by November 3 and reaches your county clerk by the start of canvass on Monday, November 9.
From the West Virginia Secretary of State .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
