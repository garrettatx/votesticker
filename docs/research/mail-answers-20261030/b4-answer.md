Leads only, condensed.

- MS: postmark by Nov 3, received by Nov 10 (SOS Oct 26 notice); county handbook: "It is illegal to hand deliver absentee ballots in Mississippi." (older handbook). Tracker: My Election Day ("track your absentee/affidavit ballot", sos.ms.gov/yall-vote). Not on list: affidavit ballot ("The voter's name is not in the pollbook", 2026 handbook). No EDR. Forgot-ID affidavit: show ID by Nov 10.
- RI: received by 8 p.m. by Board of Elections or official drop box (open 24 hours); hand-deliver to the Board or give to someone you trust; BallotTrax via vote.ri.gov. Provisional if name not on e-poll book; status online 48 hours after the election with Ballot ID number. EDR only for President in presidential years.
- KY: received by 6 p.m. local; USPS recommended mailing by Oct 27; county drop boxes; designated family/household. Tracker: Voter Information Center. Provisional federal offices only when name not on roster and status can't be determined. No EDR.
- IA: received by 8 p.m. CT; hand deliver to county auditor up to 8 p.m.; "Voted absentee ballots cannot be delivered to the polling place on Election Day - only to your county auditor's office."; surrender at polls or vote provisional. Tracker: Track Your Absentee Ballot. Not on list: provisional; or same-day registration at polls with photo ID and proof of residence.
- NH: received by 5 p.m. Nov 3 by mail or delivery agent; voter in person by 5 p.m. Nov 2. Tracker app.sos.nh.gov. EDR at the polls with proof of identity, age, citizenship, domicile; affidavit rules conflict (2024 memo ended affidavits). Oct 22: U.S. Supreme Court stayed a district court decision on student IDs; SOS: "Student ID Cards cannot be used to prove identity when registering to vote or when obtaining a ballot on election day."
- HI: received (not postmarked) by 7 p.m. Nov 3; place of deposit or voter service center. Tracker BallotTrax. Provisional if name not on list; check via county or Office of Elections; same-day registration at voter service centers.
- NV: postmark Nov 3, received by 5 p.m. Nov 7; Clark County vote centers take mail ballots 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Tracker MyBallot.NV.gov. Same-day registration with a provisional ballot.
- ME: received by 8 p.m.; municipal office or drop box. Tracker: Absentee Ballot Request Status. Challenged ballot; same-day registration during closed period, cast a challenged ballot.
- DE: received by 8 p.m.; "Ballots cannot be returned at Early Voting Sites or Polling Places."; someone may take it to the county office. Tracker iVote. Provisional federal offices only; status by unique number.
- MO: received by 7 p.m.; voter or 2nd-degree relative to the election authority. No statewide tracker; St. Louis County MyBallotTracking. Provisional stub explains status check.
- SD: return by close of polls with time to reach the precinct; "Do not return absentee ballot to your polling place"; messenger allowed. Tracker: VIP (older guide). Provisional per 2026 precinct manual.
- IL: postmark by Nov 3, received by Nov 17; issuing election authority or its drop box by 7 p.m. (Will County); grace period/Election Day registration with two IDs (Cook). Trackers: Chicago Your Voter Information, suburban Cook Your Voter Information, DuPage BallotTrax, Lake Voter Power, Will County Track My Vote By Mail Ballot. No statewide tracker.
