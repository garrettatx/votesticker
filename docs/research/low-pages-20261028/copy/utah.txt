When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Utah runs Tuesday, October 20 to Friday, October 30. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Every county holds early voting; times and locations vary, and a county can extend it through Monday, November 2. Utah also mails every voter a ballot.
Election Day hours: Polling places are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Utah also mails ballots to voters. Your mail ballot must reach the county clerk by 8 p.m. on November 3, and a postmark isn't enough.
ID: Yes, to vote in person. Show one photo ID, such as a Utah driver's license or a U.S. passport, or two documents that together prove your name and address.

Where You Can Vote
Your ballot comes in the mail, but you can also vote in person: early from October 20 to 30, or on Election Day from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Every county has at least one Election Day polling location.
Look up early voting locations and times, drop boxes and your Election Day polling location at vote.utah.gov. Your county clerk can also tell you where to vote or drop off your ballot.
Registering for the first time and voting by mail? Your county clerk may ask you to put a copy of your ID in the return envelope with your ballot. Check the instructions that come with it.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Utah driver license
ID card issued by the state of Utah or the U.S. government
Utah concealed carry permit
U.S. passport
U.S. military ID card
Tribal ID card, Bureau of Indian Affairs card or tribal treaty card (no photo needed)
Your photo ID has to be current, not expired.
No photo ID? Bring two documents with your name that together show where you live, such as a birth certificate, a Social Security card, a government check or paycheck from the last 90 days, a Medicaid or Medicare card, or your current Utah vehicle registration.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Every active voter gets a ballot in the mail. County clerks mail them between October 13 and October 27, 2026. If yours doesn't arrive soon after, call your county clerk's office right away.
Sign the affidavit on the return envelope and write the last four digits of your Utah driver license or state ID card.
Drop boxes close at 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Find the one closest to you at vote.utah.gov.
If your signature or ID number is in question, your county clerk will contact you with an affidavit to sign. It must reach the clerk by noon on the last business day before your county's canvass, and then your ballot counts.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Save the campaigning for later. Within 150 feet of the building where you vote, or where a ballot drop box sits, no one can try to sway votes by word or in print, hand out cards or flyers, or gather petition signatures.
In line at 8 p.m.? Stay in line. At a polling place you can still vote, and at a ballot drop box you can still drop off your sealed ballot.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Utah sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Utah on Election Day?
Polling places are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Utah also mails ballots to voters. Your mail ballot must reach the county clerk by 8 p.m. on November 3, and a postmark isn't enough.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
Do I need ID to vote in Utah?
Yes, to vote in person. Show one photo ID, such as a Utah driver's license or a U.S. passport, or two documents that together prove your name and address.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
Can I still register to vote in Utah?
Yes. The regular deadline, for registrations to reach your county clerk, was 5 p.m. on October 23, 2026. You can still register in person during early voting or on Election Day with two forms of ID.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Utah?
Yes. Utah law lets you photograph your own ballot and share it. Photographing someone else's ballot at a polling place is a misdemeanor.
From the Utah Election Code .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Utah?
Drop box or polling place: by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Mail: your county clerk must receive it by 8 p.m. that day. A postmark isn't enough, and ballots that arrive after November 3 don't count.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
What is a provisional ballot in Utah, and does it count?
If you register after October 23, you vote a provisional ballot. If you vote a provisional ballot without showing ID, you have until Monday, November 9, 2026, to show it to your county clerk.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Utah?
You can still vote. Anyone in line at a polling place by 8 p.m. on Election Day gets to vote, and anyone in line at a drop box by 8 p.m. with a sealed ballot gets to drop it in.
From the Utah Election Code .
How do I track my mail ballot in Utah?
Sign up at vote.utah.gov to be told when your ballot is sent and counted, or check its status there anytime.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Utah?
A mailed ballot counts only if your county clerk receives it by 8 p.m. on November 3, whatever the postmark. If it's late, use a drop box or take it to a polling location instead.
From the Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office .

Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.

Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
