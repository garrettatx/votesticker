Our sticker section: For 2026, the Secretary of State unveiled a limited-edition America 250 "I Voted" sticker for the nation's 250th birthday. Each of Ohio's 88 county boards of elections gets a limited supply for this election. Ohio's earlier sticker came from a 2019 contest for students, won by Emily, a senior at Teays Valley High School in Pickaway County. Q: Do I get an I voted sticker if I vote early in Ohio? Yes, while supplies last. If you vote in person, early or on Election Day, you get the America 250 "I Voted" sticker from your county's limited supply. Q: I voted by mail in Ohio. How do I get a sticker? Cuyahoga County's board of elections offers a digital I Voted! sticker for people who voted by mail. Save it and share it.
