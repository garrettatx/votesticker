Drop Boxes and Voting Centers
Rather vote in person? Go to any voter service and polling center in your county with ID, until 7 p.m. on Election Day. If you vote at a center outside your home county, you only get the statewide races.
Enter your registered address to find drop boxes and voting locations near you. You can return your ballot to any 24-hour drop box in the state.
Registered for the first time recently? You may need to send a copy of your ID with your mail ballot. Your county clerk includes instructions with the ballot.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Colorado driver's license or Colorado ID card
U.S. passport or passport card
U.S. military ID or veteran ID card with your photo
Government employee ID with your photo (federal, Colorado or local)
Colorado college or university student ID with your photo
Tribal ID card
Medicare or Medicaid card
Certified copy of your U.S. birth certificate or naturalization papers
A utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, government check or other government document from the last 60 days with your name and address

Returning Your Ballot
Every active voter is mailed a ballot. Ballots went out October 2 to 9. Didn't get one, or made a mistake, damaged or lost it? Ask your county clerk for a replacement, or vote in person at a voter service and polling center.
Sign the envelope when you return your mail ballot. If you're not sure it will arrive in time, drop it off in person.
Drop boxes are open 24/7 and under video surveillance. A team of election judges from both parties collects the ballots regularly. Get yours in by 7 p.m. on Election Day.
Signature missing or doesn't match, or an ID copy missing? Your county will send a letter. Fix it by following the letter, or on your phone with Colorado BallotCure, by 11:59 p.m. on Thursday, November 12.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Colorado sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When is the ballot deadline in Colorado?
Colorado votes by mail. Your ballot must reach your county clerk by 7 p.m. on November 3, and postmarks don't count. Voter service and polling centers are open for in-person voting until 7 p.m. that day.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Colorado?
If you vote in person, yes. Most people bring a Colorado driver's license or ID card, and many other IDs are accepted.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Colorado?
Yes, in person. Online and mail registration to get a ballot by mail closed October 26, 2026. You can still register and vote in person at a voter service and polling center through Election Day.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Colorado?
Yes. A 2017 law lets you show your voted ballot to others, including in a photo. County clerks can set reasonable limits on photos at voting centers.
From leg.colorado.gov .
What is a provisional ballot in Colorado, and does it count?
If you register on Election Day but don't have ID that can be verified, you can vote a provisional ballot. After Election Day, election officials check that you're eligible, and if you are, it's counted.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Colorado?
You can still vote. Everyone in line at their polling location by 7 p.m. gets to vote, no matter how long it takes.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
How do I track my mail ballot in Colorado?
Sign up for BallotTrax at ballottrax.coloradosos.gov to follow your ballot from sent to accepted. You can also log in to your voter record at GoVoteColorado.gov to see whether your ballot was mailed.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Colorado?
Postmarks don't count in Colorado. Your ballot has to be in your county clerk's hands by 7 p.m. on Election Day, so in the last week, use a drop box or voter center.
From the Colorado Secretary of State .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
