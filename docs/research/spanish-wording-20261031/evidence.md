## Prompt (Deep Research)

polls close" (vote.gov/es, EAC glossaries, TX, CA, AZ, FL, NYC, LA County, Miami-Dade, Harris, Bexar), with URL and
exact phrase; (2) what official Spanish or bilingual "I Voted" stickers print, with office, year, exact text and
URL, including posts from October 24 to 31, 2026, and Instagram's or Facebook's Spanish sticker.
