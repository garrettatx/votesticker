When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Michigan runs Saturday, October 24 to Sunday, November 1. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Every community is open at least these days; some add more. Look up your site at mi.gov/earlyvoting.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time.
ID: If you have a photo ID, such as a driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a student ID, you'll be asked to show it. If you don't have one, you can sign an affidavit and vote.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your assigned early voting site, or one of several if your community has more. You put your ballot straight into the tabulator, like on Election Day. On Election Day, go to your assigned polling place between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.
Look up your assigned early voting site and your Election Day polling place at Michigan.gov/Vote. You can only vote early at a site you're assigned to.
New to Michigan, or moved? Go to your city or township clerk's office with proof of residency, like a utility bill or bank statement, to register. You can vote an absentee ballot right there, or head to your polling place if there's time.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Driver's license or state ID from Michigan or another state
Federal, state, county or local government photo ID
U.S. passport
Military photo ID
School or college ID
Tribal photo ID
Concealed pistol license
An expired Michigan driver's license or state ID still works. Any other photo ID has to be current. Your ID doesn't need your address on it.
No photo ID, or forgot it? Sign an affidavit saying you don't have one with you, and you vote a regular ballot that's counted with all the others.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered voter can vote absentee, no reason needed. Check the box on your application to join the permanent list and get a ballot for every election.
Sign and date the return envelope in the space provided, using the signature on your driver's license, state ID or voter registration. Made a mistake, like signing in the wrong place? Ask your clerk for a new ballot.
Use one of your clerk's drop boxes by 8 p.m. on Election Day. You can also put your finished absentee ballot straight into the tabulator at your early voting site, or at your polling place on Election Day (not in Detroit).
If your signature is missing or doesn't match, your clerk will reach you by phone, email or text, or by mail if they have no other way. Send back the cure form online, in person or by mail by 5 p.m. on Friday, November 6.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign shirts, hats, pins and stickers at home. Michigan bans election or campaign clothing and accessories within 100 feet of a polling place. If you show up in a shirt or button with election images or slogans, you'll be asked to cover or remove it.
Polls close at 8 p.m. If you're in line by then, stay put. You'll be allowed to vote. The same goes at closing time on each day of early voting.

Michigan and the I Voted Sticker
Michigan has a statewide I Voted sticker contest, started in 2024 by the Secretary of State's college student advisory task force. This year more than 2,000 designs came in, the public voted on finalists, and nine winners were picked in three categories. Local clerks can order the winning stickers for November.
I voted by mail in Michigan. How do I get a sticker?
Look in your ballot envelope. In the City of Northville, every absentee ballot comes with voting instructions and an "I Voted" sticker. Other clerks decide for themselves.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Michigan sources. Last checked October 25, 2026.
When are polls open in Michigan on Election Day?
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Michigan?
If you have a photo ID, such as a driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a student ID, you'll be asked to show it. If you don't have one, you can sign an affidavit and vote.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Michigan?
Yes. Online and mail registration closed October 19, 2026. You can still register in person at your city or township clerk's office through 8 p.m. on Election Day, with proof of residency.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Michigan?
Your local clerk must receive it by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. A postmark isn't enough, so in the last two weeks use a drop box or your clerk's office instead of the mail. Military and overseas deadlines differ.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Michigan, and does it count?
You may get one if your name isn't on the list, you're at the wrong polling place, or you're a first-time voter without the required ID. You then have 6 days to bring ID to your city or township clerk. It counts if officials confirm you're registered in the right place.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Michigan?
You can still vote. Everyone qualified and in line at 8 p.m. on Election Day is allowed to vote, and the same goes at closing time during early voting.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
How do I track my mail ballot in Michigan?
Go to mi.gov/myvoterinfo and check the Absentee ballot section for when your ballot was sent and whether your clerk got it back. Sign up at mi.gov/ballotalert for email updates. Ballots handed in at an early voting site or on Election Day don't show up there.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
What if I forgot to sign my ballot envelope in Michigan?
Your clerk will contact you with a cure form. Sign and return it online, in person or by mail by 5 p.m. on Friday, November 6, or your ballot stays rejected.
From Michigan Election Law .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Michigan?
In the last two weeks, mailing it isn't recommended. It has to reach your clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day, so use a drop box, your clerk's office or your early voting site instead.
From the Michigan Secretary of State .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
