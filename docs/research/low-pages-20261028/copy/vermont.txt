When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
In Vermont, you can vote early at your town or city clerk's office during business hours, until the office closes on Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Early voting started 45 days before the election, or as soon as your town had ballots.
Election Day hours: Polls open between 5 a.m. and 10 a.m., depending on the town, and close at 7 p.m. Vermont also mails a ballot to every active voter. Return it to your town clerk by the day before the election, or to your polling place by 7 p.m.
ID: Usually not. If you registered for the first time by mail or online, you must include a copy of an ID, such as a driver's license or utility bill, with your application.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your town clerk's office during business hours, or at your polling place on Election Day. You can also bring the ballot mailed to you and vote it at the polls. Don't have it with you? You can still vote after signing an Affidavit of No Ballot Cast.
Search your address on the state's lookup map to see your polling place, your town clerk's contact information and hours, and the drop box for the town and district where you normally vote. Polls open between 5 a.m. and 10 a.m., depending on the town, and all close at 7 p.m.
Registering in Vermont for the first time by mail or online? Include a copy of an ID: a photo ID such as a driver's license or passport, a current utility bill, a current bank statement or another government document.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Every active, registered voter gets a general election ballot in the mail without asking. The state mailed them by October 1. Moved, or didn't get yours? Ask your town clerk for another; you'll sign an Affidavit of No Ballot Cast.
Sign the certificate on the outside of the envelope. Your ballot isn't valid without your signature.
Use the secure drop box in the town and district where you would normally vote in person. The state's lookup map shows where it is.
Forgot to sign, or left the ballot out of the envelope? Your town clerk will let you know, and you can fix it in person, with a new ballot, or by returning a signed affidavit before the polls close on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Wearing a candidate shirt or button? Cover it up while you're inside the building where you vote. No campaign stickers, buttons or literature can be displayed in there, and the same goes for the town clerk's office during early voting.
If you're at the polling place when it closes and haven't voted yet, stay. The polls stay open long enough for you to vote.

Vermont and the I Voted Sticker
Each year the Secretary of State's office supports a contest where K-8 students design an alternative "I Voted" sticker, and the winning designs go to voters in the student's home district. This year's winners are from Burlington, so Burlington voters get them on Election Day.

When are polls open in Vermont on Election Day?
Polls open between 5 a.m. and 10 a.m., depending on the town, and close at 7 p.m. Vermont also mails a ballot to every active voter. Return it to your town clerk by the day before the election, or to your polling place by 7 p.m.

Do I need ID to vote in Vermont?
Usually not. If you registered for the first time by mail or online, you must include a copy of an ID, such as a driver's license or utility bill, with your application.
From the Vermont Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Vermont?
Yes. You can register at your town or city clerk's office during business hours before the election, or at your polling place on Election Day.
From the Vermont Secretary of State .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Vermont?
Town clerk's office or your town's drop box: before the clerk's office closes on Monday, November 2. Polling place: hand it in by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3.
From Vermont law .
What is a provisional ballot in Vermont, and does it count?
Vermont rarely uses them. If you're not on the checklist, you can register and vote that day. If you say you registered on time, you sign an affidavit and vote.
From the Vermont Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Vermont?
You can still vote. If you're at the polling place at 7 p.m. and haven't had a chance to vote, the polls stay open long enough for you to vote.
From Vermont law .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Vermont?
Log in to the Vermont Voter Portal to check your ballot's status and the date it was mailed to you.
From the Vermont Secretary of State .
What if I forgot to sign my ballot envelope in Vermont?
Your town clerk will send you a notice with an affidavit to sign. Get it back before the polls close on Election Day by mail or in person, or by the end of the business day before the election if you send it electronically.
From Vermont law .
How do I make it my profile picture?

When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
