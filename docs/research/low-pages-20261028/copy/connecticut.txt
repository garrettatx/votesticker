Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your town's designated early voting location, or at your assigned polling place on Election Day. Every town has at least one early voting site, and anyone registered in New Britain, Windham, New Haven, Mansfield or Danbury can also vote early at the state university campus site in that town.
On Election Day, vote at your assigned polling place. For early voting, check your town's website or MyVote.CT.gov for your town's early voting location. Most towns have just one or two.
Voting for the first time in Connecticut after registering by mail? If there's a mark next to your name on the voter list, bring a current photo ID with your name and address, or a copy of a current utility bill, bank statement, government check, paycheck or government document that shows them.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any Connecticut voter can vote by absentee ballot. New for 2026, you don't need a reason such as illness or being out of town.
You can return your ballot in an official ballot drop box. Only you, a spouse, child, parent or sibling who lives with you, your designee if you're ill or disabled, a police officer or a registrar of voters can mail it or put it in a drop box for you.
Forgot to sign the envelope? Your Town Clerk will try to contact you so you can fix it before the polls close on Election Day. Put your phone number and email on your application so they can reach you.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
No campaigning within 75 feet of any outside entrance to the polling place, or in the hallways leading to it. That means no asking for votes, handing out ads or flyers, or hanging around in that zone.
If you're in line by 8 p.m., you can vote, and your vote will be counted.

Can I still register to vote in Connecticut?
Yes. The regular deadline was October 16, 2026, but you can register and vote the same day during early voting, October 19 to November 1, or on Election Day at your town's Same Day Registration location.

When is the mail ballot deadline in Connecticut?
Mailing it or using a drop box? Your Town Clerk must receive it by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Bringing it to the Town Clerk yourself? Do it by close of business on Monday, November 2, 2026.
From the Connecticut Secretary of the State .
What is a provisional ballot in Connecticut, and does it count?
If you registered to vote but your name isn't on the voter list, you can vote a provisional ballot. You'll sign a statement at the polling place saying you're qualified to vote there and haven't already voted in this election.
From the Connecticut Secretary of the State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Connecticut?
You can still vote. As long as you're in line by 8 p.m., you can vote, and an election official stands at the end of the line to close it.
From the Connecticut General Statutes .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Connecticut?
Check your absentee ballot's status with the Secretary of the State's Voter Registration Lookup, or call your Town Clerk.
