When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
In Wisconsin, you can vote an absentee ballot in person at your city, village or town clerk's office, starting as early as Tuesday, October 20, and no later than Sunday, November 1. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Each city, village and town sets its own days and hours, and some end sooner. Check with your clerk before you go.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Absentee ballots must reach your municipal clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day.
ID: Yes. Show an acceptable photo ID, such as a Wisconsin driver's license, a free state ID card, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot and must show ID afterward.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early as an in-person absentee voter at your municipal clerk's office or another site it names, with your photo ID. You vote right there and hand the sealed ballot to the clerk's staff. On Election Day, vote at your polling place from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
In-person absentee voting is at your municipal clerk's office or a site it names. Each city, village and town sets its own dates and hours, listed on MyVote. On Election Day, find your polling place on MyVote.
New to Wisconsin, or moved or changed your name? Fill out a registration form at your polling place and bring proof of residence. You don't need photo ID to register, but you'll show it before you get a ballot.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Wisconsin DOT driver's license, even if driving privileges are revoked or suspended
Wisconsin DOT ID card
Military ID from a U.S. uniformed service
U.S. passport
ID from a federally recognized tribe in Wisconsin
Photo ID from a Wisconsin accredited college or university with issue date, signature and an expiration date no more than two years out
Veteran's photo ID from the Veterans Health Administration (unexpired)
Certificate of naturalization issued in the last two years
Wisconsin DOT driving receipt or ID card receipt (valid 45 days)
A Wisconsin driver's license or state ID, military ID or U.S. passport still works if it expired after the last general election, November 5, 2024. The address on your ID doesn't have to be current.
No photo ID with you? You can vote a provisional ballot. It counts if you bring your ID to the polling place by 8 p.m., or to your municipal clerk by 4 p.m. on Friday, November 6.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered Wisconsin voter can vote absentee, for any reason. Include a copy of your photo ID with your request.
Mark your ballot in front of one adult U.S. citizen witness, then sign the certificate on the envelope. Your witness signs and writes their address too. A ballot with the witness address missing can't be counted.
Mail it, or drop it off at your clerk's office, your polling place or your community's absentee counting location. It has to be there by 8 p.m. on Election Day.
If the certificate on your envelope is filled out wrong or missing, your clerk may send the ballot back to you to fix, when there's time to correct it and return it by 8 p.m. on Election Day.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Wisconsin sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Wisconsin on Election Day?
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Absentee ballots must reach your municipal clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day.
From MyVote Wisconsin .
Do I need ID to vote in Wisconsin?
Yes. Show an acceptable photo ID, such as a Wisconsin driver's license, a free state ID card, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot and must show ID afterward.
From the Wisconsin Elections Commission .
Can I still register to vote in Wisconsin?
Yes. Online and mail registration closed October 14, 2026. You can register at your clerk's office until 5 p.m. on October 30, or at your polling place on Election Day. Bring proof of where you live.
From MyVote Wisconsin .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Wisconsin?
No. Wisconsin law makes it an offense to show your marked ballot to anyone, and a photo you share would do that.
From Wisconsin law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Wisconsin?
Your municipal clerk must have it in hand by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. A postmark doesn't count. Mailing it? The Elections Commission says to send it at least 10 days before Election Day.
From the Wisconsin Elections Commission .
What is a provisional ballot in Wisconsin, and does it count?
If you don't have a photo ID, you can vote a provisional ballot. Bring your ID to the polling place by 8 p.m., or to your municipal clerk by 4 p.m. the Friday after the election, and it counts. Miss the deadline and it doesn't.
From the Wisconsin Elections Commission .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Wisconsin?
You can still vote. Anyone waiting in line when polls close at 8 p.m. is allowed to vote.
From Wisconsin law .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Wisconsin?
Track your request on MyVote.wi.gov to see that it's approved, your ballot was sent, and it came back to be counted.
From the Wisconsin Elections Commission .
