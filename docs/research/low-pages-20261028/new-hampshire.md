Voting before Election Day

New Hampshire does not offer general, no-excuse early voting. Eligible voters can vote absentee; the qualifying reasons include absence from town, disability, religious observance, and employment obligations, including qualifying caregiving responsibilities. Your page gets this basic distinction right.

Voter ID: distinguish voting from registration

For checking in to vote, the accepted forms listed in official law include:

A driver’s license issued by any state or the federal government.

A qualifying New Hampshire identification card or another state’s motor-vehicle-agency nondriver ID.

U.S. armed services identification.

A U.S. passport or passport card.
nh

Registration deadline and same-day registration

New Hampshire permits registration at the polling place on Election Day. Advance registration closes after the municipality’s last supervisors-of-the-checklist session, generally 6–13 days before the election; there is not one statewide October deadline that applies to every town.
