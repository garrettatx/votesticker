Where You Can Vote
Vote early at any early voting center in your county, no reason needed, with your photo ID. On Election Day, vote at the polling place for your precinct.
Early voting is at any early voting center in your county. On Election Day, vote at the polling place for your precinct. Your polling place may have changed since your voter card was printed, so check MyscVOTES before you go.
Moved and didn't update your registration? If you moved within your precinct, update your address at your polling place and vote a regular ballot. If you moved to another precinct in your county, or to another county within 30 days of the election, go to your county voter registration office, change your address and vote a regular ballot there.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
South Carolina driver's license, standard or REAL ID
South Carolina DMV ID card, standard or REAL ID, including a Concealed Weapons Permit
South Carolina voter registration card with photo
Federal military ID, including Department of Defense photo IDs and the VA benefits card
U.S. passport or passport card
Your photo ID has to be current and valid. No ID? Registered voters can get a free photo voter registration card at the county voter registration office, even on Election Day.
Forgot your photo ID? Vote a provisional ballot, and show your ID at your county voter registration office before the election is certified. Can't get a photo ID? Bring your non-photo voter registration card, sign an affidavit saying why, and vote a provisional ballot that counts unless the affidavit is shown to be false.

Who Can Vote by Mail
You need a qualifying reason to vote absentee in South Carolina. You can if you:
are 65 or older
have a physical disability
can't vote during early voting hours or on Election Day because of work
are caring for someone sick or physically disabled and can't vote during early voting or on Election Day
will be out of your county for all of early voting and while polls are open on Election Day
are confined to a jail or pretrial facility awaiting arrest or trial and can't vote during early voting or on Election Day
are in the U.S. Armed Forces or Merchant Marines, or their spouse or dependent living with them
are admitted to a hospital as an emergency patient on Election Day or within four days before it
Sign the voter's oath on the return envelope and have someone 18 or older witness your signature. You don't need a notary.
Bring your ballot to your county voter registration office, or to an early voting center during early voting, and show your photo ID. A family member or authorized representative can return it for you with an Authorized Returnee form.
Sign the oath and have it witnessed before you return your ballot. South Carolina can't count a ballot unless the oath is properly signed and witnessed.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official South Carolina sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in South Carolina on Election Day?
Polling places are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. If you're in line at 7 p.m., you can vote.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
Do I need ID to vote in South Carolina?
You'll be asked for a photo ID, such as a South Carolina driver's license or a U.S. passport. If you can't get one, bring your voter registration card and sign an affidavit.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
Can I still register to vote in South Carolina?
No, not for the November 3 election. Online registration closed October 4, 2026, and South Carolina has no same-day registration.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in South Carolina?
No. South Carolina law makes it unlawful to let anyone see your ballot.
From South Carolina law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in South Carolina?
Your county voter registration office must receive it by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Mail it at least a week before Election Day, or bring it in person with your photo ID.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
What is a provisional ballot in South Carolina, and does it count?
You vote one if you forgot your photo ID, or if you can't get one and sign a reasonable impediment affidavit. If you forgot it, show your ID at the county office before certification. County boards decide which provisional ballots count on the Friday after the election.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
What if I am in line when the polls close in South Carolina?
You can still vote. Anyone in line at 7 p.m. on Election Day will be allowed to vote.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
How do I track my mail ballot in South Carolina?
Check the status of your absentee request and track your ballot online at scVOTES.gov, or call your county voter registration office.
From the South Carolina Election Commission .
