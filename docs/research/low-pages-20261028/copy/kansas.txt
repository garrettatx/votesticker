Vote Early
Kansas counties start early voting between October 14 and 27. It ends at noon Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Election Day hours: Polls are open at least 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Counties can open as early as 6 a.m. and close as late as 8 p.m., so check your county's hours.

Where You Can Vote
Before Election Day, you can advance vote in person at your county election office or a satellite voting site, starting as early as 20 days before the election. On Election Day, vote at your polling place.
Look up your polling place on VoterView by entering your address. You can also check your registration and the status of your mail ballot there.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Driver's license or nondriver ID card from Kansas or another state
U.S. passport
U.S. military ID
ID card from a Native American tribe
Employee badge or ID from a city, county, state or federal government office
Student ID from an accredited college or university in Kansas
Concealed carry license from Kansas or another state
Public assistance ID card from a government office
If your ID has an expiration date, it can't be expired when you vote. If you're 65 or older, an expired photo ID works.
Forgot your ID? You'll vote a provisional ballot, and it counts once you get your photo ID to the county election office before the county canvass. Don't have a photo ID? You can get a free nondriver ID card from the Kansas Division of Motor Vehicles.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any Kansas voter can vote by mail, no excuse needed. You have to apply for a mail ballot for each election, with your driver's license number or a copy of your photo ID.
Drop your ballot in any drop box in your county, or take it to any polling place in your county. Drop boxes lock when the polls close on Election Day.
Sign the outside of your ballot envelope. If the signature doesn't match the one on file, your county election office will contact you to verify it.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign buttons and stickers at home. Within 250 feet of the entrance to a polling place or advance voting site, you can't wear or hand out labels, signs, posters, stickers or anything else that clearly backs a candidate or a side on a ballot question. A bumper sticker on the car that drives you there is fine.
Stay in line. Everyone in line when the polls close gets to vote.

What is a provisional ballot in Kansas, and does it count?
You vote a provisional ballot if you don't show a valid photo ID, or if you asked for a mail ballot but vote in person instead. A missing-ID provisional ballot counts once you get your photo ID to the county election office before the county canvass.
