When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Georgia runs Tuesday, October 13 to Friday, October 30. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Vote at any early voting site in your county. Hours vary by site; My Voter Page lists them.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. at your assigned polling place. If you're in line by 7 p.m., you can still vote.
ID: Yes. Show a photo ID, such as a Georgia driver's license, even if it's expired, a U.S. passport, or a free Georgia voter ID card from your county registrar.

Where You Can Vote
During early voting, you can vote at any early voting site in your county. If one has a line, try another. On Election Day, vote at your assigned polling place.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Georgia driver's license, even if expired
Any valid state or federal government photo ID, including a free voter ID card from your county registrar's office or the Department of Driver Services
Student ID from a Georgia public college or university
Employee photo ID from the U.S. government, the State of Georgia, or a Georgia county, city or other local government entity
U.S. passport
U.S. military photo ID
Tribal photo ID
A Georgia driver's license works even if it's expired.
Forgot your ID or can't be verified? You can ask for a provisional ballot. It counts if you resolve the problem within three days after Election Day. No photo ID at all? Your county registrar's office issues a free voter ID card.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any Georgia voter can vote absentee by mail, for any election. You don't need to be away from your county. Request it online or by mail, then return it by mail or in a drop box.
Drop boxes are inside early voting locations and open only during early voting hours. On Election Day, bring your ballot to your county registrar's office by 7 p.m.
If your absentee ballot is rejected, your county elections office will contact you with a form to fix ("cure") your ballot envelope.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign shirts and hats at home. Campaigning is against the law within 150 feet of a polling place, and that includes shirts, hats, jackets or other clothing with a candidate's name, a party or a slogan, and signs on your car.
Take your sticker selfie outside the polling area. Georgia doesn't allow photos of your ballot, including an absentee ballot, but pictures outside the polling area are fine.
In line by 7 p.m.? Stay put. You can still vote.

When are polls open in Georgia on Election Day?
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. at your assigned polling place. If you're in line by 7 p.m., you can still vote.

Do I need ID to vote in Georgia?
Yes. Show a photo ID, such as a Georgia driver's license, even if it's expired, a U.S. passport, or a free Georgia voter ID card from your county registrar.
From the Georgia Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Georgia?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 5, 2026, and Georgia has no Election Day registration.
From libertycountyga.gov .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Georgia?
Not at the polls. Georgia law bars using cameras or cell phones in a polling place while voting is going on, unless a poll manager allows it.
From cms.chathamcountyga.gov .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Georgia?
Your county must receive it by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. You can mail it, use a drop box at an early voting location during early voting hours, or bring it to your county registrar's office by 7 p.m. on Election Day.
From Georgia.gov .
What is a provisional ballot in Georgia, and does it count?
You vote a provisional ballot when your eligibility can't be confirmed at the polling place, for example if your name isn't on the list. It counts if your county registrar's office can confirm your eligibility within three days after Election Day.
From the Georgia Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Georgia?
You can still vote. If you're in line by 7 p.m. on Election Day, you'll be allowed to vote.
From the Georgia Secretary of State .

How do I make it my profile picture?

When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
