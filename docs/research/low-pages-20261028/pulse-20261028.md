Texas’s October 27 reminder specifically tells voters to confirm their approved ID before going. 4. Five useful, shareable additions before Election Day

These are proposed features, with the supporting voter need cited separately.

1. “Still have your mail ballot?” state cards

Create a small, shareable card with:

“Must arrive by” versus “Must be postmarked by.”

The exact date and local time.

An official return-location link.

An official tracking link, where verified.

A checked-at timestamp.

Start with Ohio and Pennsylvania: both have receipt deadlines that a generic postmark reminder would fail to communicate. Ohio also provides official return and tracking instructions.
