Where You Can Vote
Vote early at any early voting site your election authority sets up. On Election Day, go to your polling place between 6 a.m. and 7 p.m. Every election authority also runs at least one Election Day vote center where any voter in its area can vote.
Early voting: any site your election authority sets up. Your election authority is your county clerk, or a city or county board of election commissioners in places like Chicago. It posts early voting sites, dates and hours.
Registering during the grace period? Bring two forms of ID, at least one with your current address, like a driver's license, lease, utility bill or bank statement. You vote right then, in person, at the same visit.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered voter can vote by mail, no excuse needed. You can also ask to join the permanent vote by mail list and get a ballot for every election.
Sign the certification on the ballot envelope. If someone helps you mark your ballot, their name and address go on the envelope too.
Some election authorities have drop boxes, open until 7 p.m. on Election Day, no stamp needed. Check the State Board of Elections' drop box list. You can also hand it in at your election authority's office.
If your signature is missing or doesn't match, you'll get a notice by mail within two days with steps to get your ballot reinstated. You have 14 days after the election to fix it with your election authority.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Illinois sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Illinois on Election Day?
Polls are open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.
From the Illinois Election Code .
Do I need ID to vote in Illinois?
Usually not; most voters give their name and sign in. If you first registered by mail and haven't proven your identity yet, you may need to show ID.
From the Illinois Election Code .
Can I still register to vote in Illinois?
Yes. Online registration closed October 18, 2026. You can still register and vote in person at grace period sites through Election Day.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Illinois?
Drop box: by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, where your election authority has one. Mail: postmarked by November 3 and received by Tuesday, November 17, 2026. You can also hand it in at your election authority's office through Election Day.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .
What is a provisional ballot in Illinois, and does it count?
You vote a provisional ballot if, for example, your name isn't on the precinct list, your eligibility is challenged, or you registered by mail without ID and still can't show it. You can bring more proof to your election authority by close of business on the Tuesday after the election, November 10. The election authority decides within 14 days whether it counts.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Illinois?
You can still vote. Polls close at 7 p.m., and everyone in line at that time gets to vote. People who arrive after 7 p.m. can't.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .
What if I forgot to sign my ballot envelope in Illinois?
Your election authority will mail you a notice within two days with steps to fix it. You have until Tuesday, November 17, 2026 to resolve it, so check your mail after you send your ballot back.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Illinois?
A postmark isn't guaranteed the day you mail it. Close to Election Day, ask at the post office counter for a hand postmark, or use a drop box or your election authority's office instead.
From the Illinois State Board of Elections .

Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
