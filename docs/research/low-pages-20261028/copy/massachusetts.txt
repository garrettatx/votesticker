ID: Usually not. Some first-time voters who registered by mail must show a photo ID or a document like a utility bill, and poll workers may ask anyone for ID.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at any early voting location in the city or town where you're registered. It may not be your Election Day polling place. On Election Day, go to your assigned polling place, open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Some towns open as early as 5:45 a.m.
Find your Election Day polling place with Where Do I Vote. For early voting sites and hours in your community, use the state's early voting schedule tool.
Voting for the first time in Massachusetts? Bring ID with your name and the address where you're registered, like a driver's license, a recent utility bill or a lease. Forgot it? Vote a provisional ballot and come back with ID before the polls close so it counts.

What ID to Bring
Asked for ID and can't show it? If you're voting for the first time, you vote a provisional ballot and come back with ID before the polls close. For any other reason, you vote a challenged ballot. It's cast as normal and only looked at in a recount, court order or audit.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered voter can vote by mail, no reason needed. Apply online at VoteInMA.com, with the application mailed to every voter, or by email or fax with your handwritten signature.
Use a secure ballot drop box in your city or town, hand it to your local election office, or drop it at an early voting site during early voting hours. You can't drop it off at a polling place on Election Day.
Check Track My Ballot to see whether your ballot was accepted or rejected. If it was rejected, or it hasn't reached your local election office, you can still vote in person, early or on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave campaign signs, posters and flyers behind. They aren't allowed in the polling place, the building or on its grounds, or within 150 feet of the entrance door. The same rule applies at early voting sites.
Polls close at 8 p.m. If you're in line at your polling place by then, stay put. You can still vote.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Massachusetts sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Massachusetts on Election Day?
Polls close at 8 p.m. They open by 7 a.m., and some cities and towns open as early as 5:45 a.m.
From the Massachusetts General Laws .
Do I need ID to vote in Massachusetts?
Usually not. Some first-time voters who registered by mail must show a photo ID or a document like a utility bill, and poll workers may ask anyone for ID.
From the Massachusetts General Laws .
Can I still register to vote in Massachusetts?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was 5 p.m. on October 24, 2026 (or a mailed form postmarked that day), and Massachusetts has no Election Day registration.
From the Massachusetts General Laws .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Massachusetts?
No. Massachusetts law makes it a crime to let anyone see how you marked your ballot for a purpose the law doesn't allow, and a photo you share would show it.
From the Massachusetts General Laws .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Massachusetts?
Drop box or your local election office: by the close of polls at 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Mail: postmarked by November 3, with 3 extra days allowed for it to arrive. Haven't mailed it a week before Election Day? Use a drop box or hand it in.
From the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth .
What is a provisional ballot in Massachusetts, and does it count?
You vote a provisional ballot if you're not on the voter list, your information on it is wrong, or you're a first-time voter without ID. It counts if your local election office confirms you're registered, and it has 12 days after the election to decide. Call 1-800-462-VOTE after Election Day to see if yours counted.
From the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Massachusetts?
You can still vote. If you're an eligible voter in line at your polling place by 8 p.m., you can cast your ballot.
From the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Massachusetts?
Use Track My Ballot on the Secretary of the Commonwealth's site. It shows the date your ballot was mailed, the date it came back, and whether it was accepted or rejected.
From the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Massachusetts?
A mailed ballot postmarked by November 3 gets 3 extra days to arrive. Still, if you haven't mailed it a week before Election Day, use a drop box or hand it in so it counts.
From the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth .

Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
