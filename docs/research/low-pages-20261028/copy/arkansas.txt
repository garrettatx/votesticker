Vote Early
Early voting in Arkansas runs Monday, October 19 to Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Weekdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturdays 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Early voting ends at 5 p.m. Monday, November 2.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.
ID: Yes. Show a photo ID, such as an Arkansas driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a concealed carry license. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot and have until noon on the Monday after the election to show ID to your county clerk.

Where You Can Vote
In most counties you vote early at the county clerk's office. Counties with other early voting sites announce them in local newspapers. On Election Day, you vote at the polling site your county election commission assigns, based on where you live.
Your county election commission assigns your polling site based on where you live. A voter ID card from your county clerk may show your precinct, and the Secretary of State's voter lookup (Voter View) shows your polling place.
Sent in your registration? You're registered once your county clerk sends you an acknowledgment. No voter ID card from your county clerk yet? Follow up with them before Election Day.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Driver's license
Photo ID card
Concealed handgun carry license
U.S. passport
Employee badge or ID from an accredited college or university in Arkansas
U.S. military ID
Public assistance ID card with your photo
Voter verification card from your county clerk
Your ID can be expired up to four years before Election Day.
No ID with you? You can vote a provisional ballot. It counts if you bring an accepted ID to your county election commission or county clerk by noon on Monday, November 9, 2026. Don't have one at all? Your county clerk gives out a free voter verification card.

Who Can Vote by Mail
You can vote absentee in Arkansas if you:
will be unavoidably away from your polling site on Election Day
can't get to your polling site on Election Day because of illness or a physical disability
are in the Uniformed Services or merchant marines, or are the spouse or dependent of a member, and are away because of the member's active duty
live in Arkansas but are temporarily living outside the United States

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Arkansas sources. Last checked October 25, 2026.
When are polls open in Arkansas on Election Day?
Polls are open 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.
From the Arkansas Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Arkansas?
Yes. Show a photo ID, such as an Arkansas driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a concealed carry license. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot and have until noon on the Monday after the election to show ID to your county clerk.
From the Arkansas Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Arkansas?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 5, 2026, and Arkansas has no Election Day registration.
From the Arkansas Secretary of State .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Arkansas?
A mailed ballot must reach your county clerk's office by 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. You can't fax or email it.
From the Arkansas Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Arkansas, and does it count?
If your name isn't on the list, you can't meet the ID rules, or a poll watcher challenges your vote, you can vote a provisional ballot after signing a statement that you're eligible. You'll be told in writing whether it counted, and why if it didn't.
From the Arkansas Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Arkansas?
You can still vote. Poll workers let everyone already in line at 7:30 p.m. vote.
