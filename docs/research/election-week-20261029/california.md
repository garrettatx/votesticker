## Election-week voter facts

All sources below are official **.gov** pages or documents. Where an official source presents a deadline in a table rather than a sentence, the exact table wording and its date are identified separately instead of inventing a quotation.

### Missing name or registration

**1. Name missing from the list: request a provisional ballot.** Eligibility still determines whether it counts. [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-bill-rights)

> “You will vote using a provisional ballot.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-bill-rights)

> “Your vote will be counted if elections officials determine that you are eligible to vote.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-bill-rights)

URL: [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-bill-rights](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-bill-rights)

**2. Check a provisional ballot afterward—not just a mailed ballot.** The county can provide its status, and the state’s My Voter Status service supports provisional-ballot checks. [voterstatus.sos.ca](https://voterstatus.sos.ca.gov/)

> “Voters that cast a provisional ballot can check the status with their county elections office.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/election-cybersecurity/trusted-information)

Official status-service wording:
> “Check the status of your vote-by-mail or provisional ballot.” [voterstatus.sos.ca](https://voterstatus.sos.ca.gov/)

URLs:
- [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/election-cybersecurity/trusted-information](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/election-cybersecurity/trusted-information)
- [https://voterstatus.sos.ca.gov/](https://voterstatus.sos.ca.gov/)

**3. Missing the ordinary registration deadline does not end your options.** Same-day conditional registration runs through November 3. [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/frequently-asked-questions)

Exact calendar wording:
> “Same Day Registration available. Voters can "conditionally" register and vote a provisional ballot during this time” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/frequently-asked-questions)

**Table date:** “October 20 - November 3, 2026.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/frequently-asked-questions)

URL: [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/upcoming-elections/general-election-november-3-2026/key-dates-deadlines](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/upcoming-elections/general-election-november-3-2026/key-dates-deadlines)

### Returning a mail ballot

**7. Mailing on Election Day: obtain a hand-stamped postmark.** This is especially useful advice for someone holding a ballot during November 1–3. [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

> “If mailing on Election Day, get a hand-stamped postmark from a postal employee inside a United States Post Office.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

URL: [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail)

**8. November 10 is the receipt deadline—not permission to mail after November 3.** Both the timely postmark and receipt requirements apply. [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

> “Mailed ballots must be postmarked on or before Election Day and received not later than 7 days after Election Day.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

The November election calendar assigns **November 10, 2026** to the mailed-ballot receipt deadline. [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/frequently-asked-questions)

URLs:
- [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail)

**9. Someone else may return your ballot, but cannot receive per-ballot payment.** [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

> “Anyone may return your ballot for you, as long as they do not get paid on a per ballot basis.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

URL: [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail)

**10. Valid mail ballots count regardless of whether a contest is close.** [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)

> “All valid vote-by-mail ballots are counted in every election in California, regardless of the outcome or closeness of any race.” [sos.ca](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/vote-mail?os=)
