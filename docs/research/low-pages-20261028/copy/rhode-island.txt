When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Rhode Island runs Wednesday, October 14 to Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Vote at your city or town hall, or a designated location, during regular business hours.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. In New Shoreham, on Block Island, they open at 9 a.m.
ID: Bring a photo ID, such as a Rhode Island driver's license, a free voter ID card, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot that counts if your signature matches.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at the location your city or town designates, or at your polling place on Election Day. Already applied for a mail ballot? If you go to a polling place on Election Day, you'll vote a provisional ballot.
Find your early voting location, the nearest mail ballot drop box and your Election Day polling place at vote.ri.gov, or ask your local board of canvassers.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Rhode Island driver's license
Rhode Island voter identification card, free from the Secretary of State
U.S. passport
ID card from a U.S. school, college or university
U.S. military ID card
ID card issued by the United States or the State of Rhode Island
Government-issued medical card
Your ID can be expired up to six months.
No photo ID? You can still vote a provisional ballot. Your local board compares the signature on your application with the one on your registration, and if they match, your ballot counts.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any Rhode Island voter who'd rather not vote in person can vote by mail. Accessible mail ballots are offered too.
Seal your ballot in the certificate envelope, then sign and date it. Can't sign your name? An assistant fills out the Voter Assistant Certification.
Drop it in a 24-hour elections drop box by 8 p.m. on Election Day. Every polling place also has a drop box for mail ballots.
If there's a problem with your mail ballot envelope, you'll be notified by phone, text or mail. The Deficient Mail Ballot Attestation Form must reach the Board of Elections, not just be postmarked, by 4 p.m. on Tuesday, November 10.

Rhode Island and the I Voted Sticker
After you put your ballot in the voting machine, wear your I Voted sticker. The Department of State's 2026 voter handbook says so.
Do I get an I voted sticker if I vote early in Rhode Island?
Yes. The Department of State's early voting steps end with returning the empty secrecy folder and wearing your "I voted" sticker.
I voted by mail in Rhode Island. How do I get a sticker?
Look in your mail ballot packet. The Department of State lists an "I Voted by Mail" sticker among the items it includes.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Rhode Island sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Rhode Island on Election Day?
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. In New Shoreham, on Block Island, they open at 9 a.m.
From the Rhode Island Department of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Rhode Island?
Bring a photo ID, such as a Rhode Island driver's license, a free voter ID card, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot that counts if your signature matches.
From Rhode Island law .
Can I still register to vote in Rhode Island?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 4, 2026, and Rhode Island has no Election Day registration.
From Rhode Island law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Rhode Island?
Use a drop box by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. It's too late to count on the mail: your ballot has to reach the State Board of Elections by 8 p.m. that day. Every polling place has a drop box too.
From the Rhode Island Department of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Rhode Island, and does it count?
You vote a provisional ballot if your name isn't on the voter list, you don't have an acceptable photo ID, you applied for a mail ballot, or there's another question about your eligibility. Your local board of canvassers decides after Election Day whether it counts. Check the result at elections.ri.gov or by calling 401-222-2345.
From the Rhode Island Department of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Rhode Island?
You can still vote. Anyone waiting in line to vote at 8 p.m. gets to vote.
From Rhode Island law .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Rhode Island?
Sign up at vote.ri.gov to track your mail ballot. You'll get updates when it's mailed to you and when the Board of Elections receives it.
From the Rhode Island Department of State .

Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
