When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Wyoming runs Tuesday, October 6 to Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Vote absentee in person at your county clerk's office, no excuse needed, through the day before the election.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. If you're in line at 7 p.m., you can still vote.
ID: Yes. Show an acceptable ID, such as a Wyoming driver's license or ID card, a U.S. passport, a tribal ID, or a Medicare or Medicaid card.

Where You Can Vote
Vote absentee in person with your county clerk before Election Day, or at your polling place on Election Day. Some counties run vote centers, where you can vote at any polling place in the county; your county clerk can tell you.
Enter your address in the state's Polling Place Locator, or call your county clerk's office. Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.
Registered by mail and voting absentee in your first federal election? Put a copy of your photo ID, or a document with your name and home address, in the envelope with your ballot.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Wyoming driver's license or ID card
Tribal ID card
U.S. passport
U.S. military card
Driver's license or ID card from another state
University of Wyoming student ID
Wyoming community college student ID
Wyoming public school student ID
Medicare or Medicaid insurance card (accepted through December 31, 2029)
Wyoming concealed firearm permit
Don't have one of these? You can get a free Wyoming ID card at your local Driver Services office.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered Wyoming voter can vote absentee. You don't need to give a reason. If you mail it in, you don't need to send ID.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Save the campaigning for far from the polls. On Election Day, no campaigning of any kind, including signs and handing out literature, within 100 yards of any public entrance to the building where you vote. At absentee polling places on other days, it's 100 feet. One small bumper sticker per candidate on your parked car is fine while you vote.
Waiting to vote at 7 p.m.? Stay. You can still vote.

Wyoming and the I Voted Sticker
In 2019, for the 150th anniversary of women's suffrage in Wyoming, the Secretary of State's office ran an I Voted sticker design contest for high school juniors and seniors and college students. County clerks were to hand out the winning stickers at polling places.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Wyoming sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Wyoming on Election Day?
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. If you're in line at 7 p.m., you can still vote.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Wyoming?
Yes. Show an acceptable ID, such as a Wyoming driver's license or ID card, a U.S. passport, a tribal ID, or a Medicare or Medicaid card.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Wyoming?
Yes. Regular registration closed October 20, 2026, but you can register and vote at the same time during early absentee voting or on Election Day. Bring proof of identity, citizenship, and residence.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Wyoming?
Your county clerk's office must receive it by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Mail inside the county can take up to seven days, longer from outside it, so mail it early or bring it to the clerk's office yourself.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Wyoming, and does it count?
If you're turned away or can't prove you're eligible, you can vote a provisional ballot. For it to count, bring proof that you're eligible to your county clerk by close of business on Wednesday, November 4.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Wyoming?
You can still vote. Polls stay open until the last person in line at 7 p.m. has voted.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Wyoming?
Call your county clerk to check on your absentee ballot.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Wyoming?
It won't count. Your county clerk must receive it by 7 p.m. on Election Day, whatever the postmark. If time is short, bring it to the clerk's office in person instead.
From the Wyoming Secretary of State .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
