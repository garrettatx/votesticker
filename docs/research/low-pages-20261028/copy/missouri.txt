Where You Can Vote
From the second Tuesday before Election Day through the day before, anyone can vote absentee in person with no excuse, at a location your local election authority sets. Bring a photo ID.
Look yourself up in the Secretary of State's portal to see where you're registered and where you vote.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Missouri driver or non-driver license, not expired
Military ID, including a veteran's ID card, not expired
U.S. passport, not expired
Another photo ID from the United States or the state of Missouri, not expired or expired after the most recent general election
A Missouri driver or non-driver license that expired after the most recent general election still works.
No photo ID? If you're registered, you can vote a provisional ballot. It counts if you come back to your polling place on Election Day with a photo ID, or if your signature on the envelope matches your registration.

Who Can Vote by Mail
To vote absentee by mail in Missouri, you need one of these reasons:
you'll be away from your election authority's area on Election Day
you're sick or physically disabled on Election Day, or you're the main caregiver for someone at your address who is
you work for an election authority somewhere other than your polling place, or you're a first responder, a health care worker or in law enforcement
you're incarcerated but still qualified to vote
you're in the state's address confidentiality program for safety reasons
Sign the affidavit on your ballot envelope in front of a notary, the election official who takes your ballot, or another officer who can give oaths. You can skip the notary if you're voting absentee because of illness, physical disability or confinement.
Missouri doesn't use drop boxes for absentee ballots. Mail yours, or bring it back in person yourself or have a close relative bring it.
Fill out every statement on your ballot envelope before you send it. Missouri rejects an absentee ballot whose envelope statements aren't completed.

When are polls open in Missouri on Election Day?
Polls are open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. If you're in line at 7 p.m., you can still vote.
From the Missouri Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Missouri?
Yes. Show a photo ID, such as a Missouri driver's license or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot, which counts if you come back with ID that day or your signature matches.
From the Missouri Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Missouri?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 7, 2026, and Missouri has no Election Day registration.
From the Missouri Secretary of State .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Missouri?
No. Missouri law makes it an election offense to let anyone see your ballot so they know how you voted, and a photo you share would do that.
From Missouri law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Missouri?
Your local election authority must receive your absentee ballot by the time the polls close at 7 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3. Ballots that arrive later don't count.
From Missouri law .
What is a provisional ballot in Missouri, and does it count?
If you vote a provisional ballot, you'll get a stub from the envelope with instructions on how to check that your ballot was counted.
From the Missouri Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Missouri?
You can still vote. If you're in line at 7 p.m., you have the right to cast your ballot.

Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
