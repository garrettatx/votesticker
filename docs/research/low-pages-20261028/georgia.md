Photos: the main legal wording change

Your body copy says:

“Georgia doesn't allow photos of your ballot, including an absentee ballot…”

That is broader than the statutory language. O.C.G.A. § 21-2-568.2 prohibits photographing or recording a voted ballot. That distinction matters: “your ballot” could also mean an unmarked ballot or sample ballot. The enacted SB 202 text contains the prohibition on photographing a voted ballot.
legis.ga

Your FAQ separately describes restrictions on cameras and phones inside a polling place. That is another rule: § 21-2-413 restricts device use while voting is taking place, allows limited poll-manager permission for photography, and protects ballots, voting-machine displays and voter lists from photography.
law.justia

Use one consistent answer in both places:

Don’t photograph your marked ballot, including an absentee ballot. Cameras and phones are restricted inside the polling place. Take your sticker selfie outside, without showing anyone’s ballot.
legis.ga
