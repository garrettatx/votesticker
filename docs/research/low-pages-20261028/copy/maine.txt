Where You Can Vote
Want to vote before Election Day? Go to your town or city clerk's office and vote an absentee ballot right there, no application needed. Ballots are there 30 days before the election, and the last day is Thursday, October 29. On Election Day, vote at your town's polling place.
Every town and city has its own polling place. Look yours up with your home address in the state's Voter Information Lookup Service, or call your town office or city hall. Opening times vary by town, but every polling place closes at 8 p.m.
Not registered yet? Register in person, even on Election Day at the polls. Bring an ID and proof of where you live, such as a driver's license, a passport, a utility bill or a bank statement. If you're in line at 8 p.m., you can still register and vote.

What ID to Bring
Already registered? You don't need to show ID to get a ballot. Registering at the polls without ID or proof of where you live? You'll vote a challenged ballot, and you may be asked to show ID after the election.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered voter in Maine can vote absentee, no reason needed. You can also sign up for ongoing absentee status and get a ballot for every election automatically.
Seal your ballot in the return envelope and fill out the affidavit on it. You don't need a witness or notary unless someone helps you mark it, or a third person you named handles your ballot.
Return it by mail, in person at your town office, or in your town's absentee ballot drop box if it has one, by 8 p.m. on Election Day. Only you or an immediate family member can return it, unless you name a third person in writing.
Put your phone number or email on your absentee application. If your envelope is missing a signature, the signature doesn't match, or the affidavit isn't filled out right, your clerk will contact you about how to fix it before 8 p.m. on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign shirts and signs at home. Maine bans campaign posters, literature, stickers and apparel with a candidate's name on public property within 250 feet of the polling place entrance. If you're only there to vote, a campaign button up to 3 inches across is allowed.
A sticker selfie is fine: Maine law allows a still photo that shows only you. Anyone taking other photos or video in the voting place has to stay outside the guardrail enclosure and at least 15 feet from the voter.
Polls close at 8 p.m. If you're at the polling place at closing time, you get the chance to vote. Anyone who arrives after 8 p.m. can't.

When are polls open in Maine on Election Day?
Polls close at 8 p.m. Each town sets its opening time, between 6 a.m. and 8 a.m., or as late as 10 a.m. in towns with fewer than 500 people.
From Maine law .
Do I need ID to vote in Maine?
No, if you're already registered. You state your name and address to the election clerk. If you register in person, you must show proof of identity and residence.
From Maine law .
Can I still register to vote in Maine?
Yes. Mail and online registration closed October 13, 2026, but you can register in person with your town's registrar through Election Day, including at the polls.
From Maine law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Maine?
Your municipal clerk must receive your ballot by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Mailing it? The Secretary of State suggests sending it at least 7 days before Election Day.
From the Maine Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Maine, and does it count?
Maine calls it a challenged ballot. It's used when someone questions your eligibility on Election Day, and you can't be turned away. Challenged ballots are counted like regular ballots, and they're only reviewed if a recount could turn on them.
From the Maine Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Maine?
You can still vote. If you're in line at the polling place at 8 p.m., you can still register and vote.
From the Maine Secretary of State .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Maine?
Use the Secretary of State's online absentee ballot tracker. It shows when your clerk accepted your application, mailed your ballot and got it back. Your clerk enters the updates, so they may lag a little.
From the Maine Secretary of State .
What if I forgot to sign my ballot envelope in Maine?
If you gave a phone number or email on your application, your clerk will contact you about how to fix it before 8 p.m. on Election Day. If you didn't, a missing signature means your ballot is rejected.
From Maine law .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Maine?
Mailing it in the last week is risky. It has to reach your clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day, so drop it off at your town office or its drop box, or vote in person at the polls.
From the Maine Secretary of State .
