VoteSticker.org Offers Free Digital "I Voted" Stickers for Every State

Austin-built, nonpartisan site pairs shareable sticker designs with links to official voting information

AUSTIN, TX, UNITED STATES, [publication date, week of Nov 4] /EINPresswire.com/ -- VoteSticker.org offers free digital "I voted" stickers for all 50 states and Washington, D.C., giving voters a way to share that they took part in the November 3, 2026 general election.

Created by Garrett Digital, an Austin search marketing consultancy, the independent, nonpartisan website lets visitors save a ready-made sticker or make their own, without creating an account or giving an email address. Nothing is for sale.

The site brings a familiar tradition, the small sticker that says someone showed up to vote, to profile pictures, posts and messages. Designs include "I voted," "I voted early," "I voted by mail" and first-time voter stickers, in English and Spanish. Each fits the circular profile-picture crop, and a transparent PNG version can sit over a photo or go into a story. Custom stickers are made in the visitor's browser and are not uploaded to the site.

"I built VoteSticker.org because getting an 'I voted' sticker is a small thing people enjoy," said Garrett Nafzinger, creator of VoteSticker.org. "I wanted to make it easy to find one you like and share it online, with no signup and no email required. And if someone still needs voting information, the site points them to official sources."

Each state page lists that state's 2026 voting dates from official sources and links to its state and local election offices. The site also points to official digital stickers that election offices publish and tells the stories behind state and local sticker designs, including 2026 sticker contest winners. A history page traces "I have voted" tags back to a 1920 St. Louis school report.

VoteSticker.org is not a government website, and its designs are not official election-office stickers or proof of voting. Voters should confirm registration, polling places, hours, ID rules and deadlines with their state or local election office.

More information: https://votesticker.org

About VoteSticker.org

VoteSticker.org is a free, independent, nonpartisan website created by Garrett Digital in Austin, Texas. It offers downloadable and customizable voting sticker images with state-by-state voting information and links to official election resources.
