Election Day hours and time zones

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Polls open between 7 and 9 a.m. and close between 7 and 9 p.m., local time, depending on the county/location. The Secretary of State specifically warns against reporting one statewide opening or closing time. Voters in line at closing may still vote.
sos.nd

North Dakota uses Central and Mountain time. Most of the state uses Central; the southwest uses Mountain, with portions of Dunn, McKenzie and Sioux counties split between the zones. For this election, standard time resumes November 1, so Election Day labels should be CST or MST—not CDT or MDT.

Voter ID

Accepted forms include a North Dakota driver’s license, North Dakota non-driver ID, qualifying tribal ID or tribal documentation, and a North Dakota long-term-care certificate. Identification must establish legal name, date of birth and current North Dakota residential address. Regular and REAL ID versions are accepted; NDDOT digital IDs are not.

Registration and same-day voting

North Dakota does not require voter registration. There is no registration deadline to miss and no separate “same-day registration” process. Eligible voters establish eligibility and identity when voting.

Absentee applications and returns

Any qualified voter may vote absentee without an excuse. The online application must be completed, printed, signed and submitted; it is not a fully online ballot-request submission process.
