### Missing name: Request provisional voting

A missing name is a reason to seek help and a provisional ballot—not simply leave the polling place. The State Board’s 2026 explanation expressly covers this situation. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/03/05/state-board-releases-number-provisional-ballots-cast-county-voting-method-2026-primary-election)

**Exact quotation:**
> “A voter may cast a provisional ballot when their name does not appear on the voter registration rolls for the voting location or other questions arise about their eligibility to vote or to vote a particular ballot.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/03/05/state-board-releases-number-provisional-ballots-cast-county-voting-method-2026-primary-election)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/03/05/state-board-releases-number-provisional-ballots-cast-county-voting-method-2026-primary-election](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/03/05/state-board-releases-number-provisional-ballots-cast-county-voting-method-2026-primary-election)

**Exact quotation:**
> “Any voter in line at their assigned polling place at 7:30 p.m. will be able to vote.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/events/election-day-2026-general-election)

## Mail-ballot facts

### Receipt—not postmark—controls

For ordinary civilian absentee ballots, the deadline is **receipt by 7:30 p.m. November 3**, not mailing or postmarking by that time. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/09/02/voting-2026-general-election-begins-week-nc)

**Exact quotation:**
> “Completed absentee ballots must be received by the voter’s county board of elections by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day – Nov. 3.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/09/02/voting-2026-general-election-begins-week-nc)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/09/02/voting-2026-general-election-begins-week-nc](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2026/09/02/voting-2026-general-election-begins-week-nc)

The current instructions expressly say the former postmark grace period no longer applies. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

### No Election Day polling-place drop-off

A county elections office and an Election Day polling place are **not interchangeable ballot-return locations**. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

**Exact quotation:**
> “You may not return your ballot to a polling place on Election Day.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

### Return assistance has a disability exception

The usual restrictions on who may return someone else’s ballot do not apply in the same way when the voter needs assistance because of a disability. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

**Exact quotation:**
> “If you need assistance returning your ballot due to disability, these restrictions do not apply, and you may ask any person you choose to return your ballot.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/vote-mail/detailed-instructions-vote-mail)

### Military/overseas mail has another deadline

Qualified military and overseas voters have a mailed-ballot exception: **November 12 at 5 p.m.**, subject to the mailing-time requirement—not a civilian grace period. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/military-and-overseas-voting/faq)

**Exact quotation:**
> “Exception: If you mail the ballot, it is still timely if your county board of elections receives it by 5 p.m. ET on the business day before county canvass (if mailed by 12:01 a.m. your time on Election Day).” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/military-and-overseas-voting/faq)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/military-and-overseas-voting/faq](https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/military-and-overseas-voting/faq)

### Timely arrival can mean later counting

Distinguish **ballots received too late** from **timely ballots counted after election night**. An official explanation says ballots returned on Election Day are considered during canvass; that explanation was published in 2024, not newly for 2026. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2024/02/27/some-election-results-will-be-reported-later-usual-due-state-law-changes)

**Exact quotation:**
> “Under state law, ballots returned on Election Day are approved and added to the vote totals during the 10-day period after Election Day known as the ‘canvass.’” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2024/02/27/some-election-results-will-be-reported-later-usual-due-state-law-changes)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2024/02/27/some-election-results-will-be-reported-later-usual-due-state-law-changes](https://www.ncsbe.gov/news/press-releases/2024/02/27/some-election-results-will-be-reported-later-usual-due-state-law-changes)

The current reporting timeline also identifies provisional, qualifying military/overseas and successfully cured absentee ballots as post-election additions. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/election-results/election-night-reporting-timeline)

## Canvass and certification

### County canvass: November 13

The statutory schedule produces **Friday, November 13, 2026, at 11 a.m.**—ten days after Election Day. This is a scheduled legal milestone, not a claim that certification has already occurred. [ncleg](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

**Exact quotation:**
> “The county board of elections shall meet at 11:00 A.M. on the tenth day after every election to complete the canvass of votes cast and to authenticate the count in every ballot item in the county by determining that the votes have been counted and tabulated correctly.” [ncleg](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

### State canvass: November 24

The statutory statewide schedule produces **Tuesday, November 24, 2026, at 11 a.m.**, three weeks after Election Day. [ncleg](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

**Exact quotation:**
> “After each general election, the State Board of Elections shall meet at 11:00 A.M. on the Tuesday three weeks after election day to complete the canvass of votes cast in all ballot items within the jurisdiction of the State Board of Elections and to authenticate the count in every ballot item in the county by determining that the votes have been counted and tabulated correctly.” [ncleg](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_163/gs_163-182.5.html)

### Election-night numbers are unofficial

Certification follows canvass and audit work; a November 4 result should not be described as certified merely because all precincts appear to have reported. [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/election-results/historical-election-results-data)

**Exact quotation:**
> “Results are certified at the end of canvass.” [ncsbe](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/election-results/historical-election-results-data)

**Official URL:** [https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/election-results/historical-election-results-data](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/election-results/historical-election-results-data)
