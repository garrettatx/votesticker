6. Topics for Perplexity Research

  • "i voted early sticker" is the biggest single query (about 110 impressions across TX, NC, CA, NY, SC, MI, MO and others), ranking 5 to 8. Research: what early voters actually get at the polls in each state, which states hand out "I voted early" stickers, and what people search when they vote early.
  • "how to get i voted sticker" (TX, OR, WA). Research: where to get a physical sticker in each state, especially mail-ballot states like Oregon and Washington, and whether counties send stickers with mail ballots.
  • "[state] i voted sticker 2026" (CA, LA, TX). Research: each state's or big county's 2026 sticker design, design contests, and news coverage of them.
  • "when is early voting in [state] 2026" (Idaho). Research: early voting dates, hours and locations per state, which the pages partly carry in their titles.
  • Home page "i voted stickers by state" (65 impressions, position 7 to 8). Research: what the ranking pages offer on a by-state roundup (galleries of official stickers, history, sticker trivia).
