Where You Can Vote
Vote at any Vote Center in DC, during early voting or on Election Day, wherever you live in the District. There's at least one early voting Vote Center in every ward.
First time voting in DC and registered by mail? Bring a copy of a current government photo ID, utility bill, bank statement, government check or paycheck with your name and address, unless you sent one when you registered. Without it, you vote a special ballot.
From the D.C. Code . Last checked October 26, 2026.
Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered DC voter can vote by mail, for any reason.
Put it in a ballot drop box or bring it to a Vote Center by 8 p.m. on Election Day.
If there's a problem with your mail ballot, such as a signature that doesn't match, the DC Board of Elections will send you a letter, and BallotTrax will alert you if you signed up. Return the signed certificate from the letter by email, by mail or in person by the deadline the letter gives.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official District of Columbia sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Washington, DC on Election Day?
Vote centers are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on November 3, and you can vote at any of them. Every registered voter also gets a ballot by mail. Mail it postmarked by November 3, or use a drop box by 8 p.m.
From the DC Board of Elections .
Do I need ID to vote in Washington, DC?
Usually not. If you registered by mail or online for the first time, you may need to show ID with your name and address, such as a photo ID or a utility bill.
From the DC Board of Elections .
Can I still register to vote in Washington, DC?
Yes. Online and mail registrations had to arrive by October 13, 2026, but you can register and vote in person during early voting or on Election Day. Bring proof of where you live.
From the DC Board of Elections .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Washington, DC?
Drop box or Vote Center: by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Mail: postmarked on or before November 3 and received by the Board of Elections within 10 days after Election Day.
From the D.C. Code .
What is a provisional ballot in Washington, DC, and does it count?
DC calls it a special ballot. If your name is missing from the list, or your name or address is wrong, you can still vote one. The Board reviews it, you can call a toll-free line to hear its decision, and you get a hearing 8 to 10 days after the election if it says no.
From the D.C. Code .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Washington, DC?
You can still vote. Anyone in line at a vote center when the polls close gets to vote.
From the DC Municipal Regulations .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Washington, DC?
Track your mail-in ballot on the Board of Elections website, from the day it's mailed to you to when it's received and accepted.
From the D.C. Code .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Washington, DC?
A mailed ballot counts if it's postmarked on or before November 3 and reaches the Board within 10 days. Mailing it late? Use a drop box or a Vote Center by 8 p.m. on Election Day instead.
From the D.C. Code .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
