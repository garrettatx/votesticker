Where You Can Vote
Vote early at an early voting or in-person absentee location in your county until October 30. On Election Day, go to your assigned voting location, based on your home address, from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Look up your assigned Election Day voting location on VoteIdaho.gov. For early voting, find your county in the early voting chart or ask your county elections office.
Registering at the polls? Bring a current photo ID and proof of residence, such as a lease, mortgage or rental agreement. Register using your main Idaho home address.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Idaho driver's license or ID card
U.S. passport or federal photo ID card
Concealed weapons license issued by an Idaho county sheriff
No photo ID? If you're registered, sign a Personal Identification Affidavit with your name and address, swearing to who you are. You then get a regular ballot, counted with all the others.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered Idaho voter can request an absentee ballot by mail, no reason needed. You need a new request each calendar year.
Sign the outside of the return envelope. Elections staff verify every signature. Mailing it? Add postage.
Use an official elections drop box or take it to your county elections office by 8 p.m. on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign gear at home. Voting locations are neutral spaces, so don't wear or bring anything that promotes a candidate, issue or party on the ballot.
Polls close at 8 p.m. If you're in line by then, stay put. You can still vote.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Idaho sources. Last checked October 25, 2026.
When are polls open in Idaho on Election Day?
Polls are open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time, and some counties open at 7 a.m. Idaho has two time zones.
From legislature.idaho.gov .
Do I need ID to vote in Idaho?
You'll be asked for a photo ID, such as an Idaho driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a tribal ID. If you don't have one, you can sign an identity affidavit instead.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
Can I still register to vote in Idaho?
Yes. Preregistration closed October 23, 2026. You can still register and vote in person during early voting or on Election Day with a photo ID and proof of where you live.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Idaho?
Mail it with a stamp, drop it in an official elections drop box, or bring it to your county elections office. Your county clerk must receive it by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
What is a provisional ballot in Idaho, and does it count?
Idaho doesn't use provisional ballots. If you're not registered, register at early voting or at your polling place on Election Day with a photo ID and proof of residence.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Idaho?
You can still vote. If you're in line by 8 p.m. on Election Day, you may vote even after the polls close.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Idaho?
Check your voter record on VoteIdaho.gov to see whether your county clerk has mailed your absentee ballot and whether your county received it back.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .
Is it too late to mail my ballot in Idaho?
Your county clerk must receive it by 8 p.m. on Election Day. Running late? Use an official drop box or take it to your county elections office instead of mailing it.
From the Idaho Secretary of State (VoteIdaho.gov) .

After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
