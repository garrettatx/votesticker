Where You Can Vote
Before Election Day, any registered voter can vote absentee in person at the county auditor's office or at a satellite location, if your county has one. On Election Day, vote at the polling place for the precinct where you live.
Iowa law says you vote at the polling place for the precinct where you live. Not sure where that is? Look it up by ZIP code or ask your county auditor.
Not registered yet? Go to the polling place for your current address on Election Day and register there. Bring ID and proof of where you live, like a lease, utility bill or bank statement from the last 45 days. Without them, a registered voter from your precinct can attest for you.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Iowa driver's license, expired no more than 90 days
Iowa non-operator ID, expired no more than 90 days
U.S. passport, not expired
U.S. military ID or veteran ID, not expired
Iowa Voter Identification Card, signed
Tribal ID card or document, signed, with a photo, not expired
An Iowa driver's license or non-operator ID still works up to 90 days after it expires. A passport, military or veteran ID, or tribal ID must not be expired.
No ID from the list? You can prove who you are and where you live with the documents used for Election Day registration. Otherwise you'll be offered a provisional ballot, and you can bring ID until the county canvass, the Monday after Election Day.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered voter in Iowa can vote absentee, by mail or in person. You don't need a reason.
If your county auditor finds a problem with your returned envelope, they'll let you know. You can fix the affidavit in person at the auditor's office, ask for a replacement ballot or vote at the polls, until polls close on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Keep campaigning at least 300 feet from the polling place door. On Election Day, Iowa law bans electioneering, posting signs and asking for votes on polling place grounds or within 300 feet of an outside door that leads to the polls.
Take your sticker selfie without getting in other voters' way. Iowa bans photos and showing your voted ballot only when it's for an illegal purpose or it interferes with other voters or the polling place.
If you're at your precinct's polling place when it closes, stay. You can still vote.

When is the mail ballot deadline in Iowa?
Your county auditor's office must receive your ballot by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Mail it or hand deliver it to the auditor. Voted absentee ballots can't be dropped off at your polling place.
From Voter Ready Iowa .
What is a provisional ballot in Iowa, and does it count?
You're offered a provisional ballot if you were already sent an absentee ballot, don't show ID when it's required, or are challenged by another voter. You get a notice that day saying why and whether you need to bring more documents.
From the Iowa Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Iowa?
You can still vote. Every voter who is at their precinct's polling place when it closes gets to vote.
