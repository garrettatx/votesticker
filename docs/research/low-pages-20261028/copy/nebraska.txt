Vote Early
You can vote early in person at your county election office from Monday, October 5, through Monday, November 2. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Each county election office sets its own hours; call yours before you go.
Election Day hours: Polls are open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the Central Time Zone and 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. in the Mountain Time Zone.
ID: Yes. Show a photo ID with your name and photo, such as a Nebraska driver's license, a state ID, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot.

Where You Can Vote
On Election Day, vote at your assigned polling place. Eleven counties vote entirely by mail: Boone, Cedar, Cherry, Clay, Dawes, Dixon, Garden, Knox, Merrick, Morrill and Stanton. There your ballot comes in the mail, and you can still vote in person at the county election office on Election Day.
Votercheck shows your assigned polling place. You can also ask your county clerk or election commissioner. Early voting in person happens at your county election office.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Nebraska driver's license
Nebraska state ID from the DMV
Nebraska college ID, public or private
ID from a Nebraska state, county, city, public school or other political subdivision
U.S. passport
Military ID
Tribal ID
A hospital, assisted-living, nursing home, hospice or care facility record with your name and photo
Your ID can be expired. It needs your name and photo, but not an address or an expiration date.
Forgot it? Go home and get it before polls close, or vote a provisional ballot and show your ID in person at your county election office by the Tuesday after the election. If a disability, illness, missing documents or a religious objection keeps you from getting an ID, fill out a Reasonable Impediment Certification.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered Nebraska voter can vote early by mail. You don't need a reason.
Seal and sign the return envelope. If someone helped you mark your ballot, that person signs it too.
Every Nebraska county has at least one secure drop box. Use one in your own county by 8 p.m. Central (7 p.m. Mountain) on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign buttons, hats and shirts at home. Bringing anything with a candidate's name, likeness or logo, or a ballot measure's number or title, into the polling place on Election Day is against the law. No campaigning or handing out political literature within 200 feet, either.
In line when polls close? Stay in line. Anyone at the polling place or in line at closing time gets to vote.

Questions People Ask
Answers come from official Nebraska sources. Last checked October 26, 2026.
When are polls open in Nebraska on Election Day?
Polls are open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the Central Time Zone and 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. in the Mountain Time Zone.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
Do I need ID to vote in Nebraska?
Yes. Show a photo ID with your name and photo, such as a Nebraska driver's license, a state ID, or a U.S. passport. Without one, you vote a provisional ballot.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
Can I still register to vote in Nebraska?
No, not for the November 3 election. In-person registration at county election offices closed at 6 p.m. on October 23, 2026, online and mail registration closed October 16, and Nebraska has no Election Day registration.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
Can I take a picture of my ballot in Nebraska?
Yes. Nebraska law lets you photograph your own marked ballot and share the photo. No one may pressure you to show your ballot.
From Nebraska law .
When is the mail ballot deadline in Nebraska?
Get it to your county election office by 8 p.m. Central (7 p.m. Mountain) on Tuesday, November 3. A postmark isn't enough: it has to arrive by then, so if time is short, use a drop box in your county or bring it in.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Nebraska, and does it count?
If you moved within your county or changed your name, you may be asked to vote a provisional ballot. Seven business days after the election, check whether it counted on Votercheck or by calling 1-888-727-0007.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Nebraska?
You can still vote. Anyone at the polling place or in line when polls close gets to vote. Anyone who arrives after closing can't.
From Nebraska law .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in Nebraska?
Look yourself up on Votercheck to see when your ballot was sent, when it came back and its status.
From the Nebraska Secretary of State .
How do I make it my profile picture?
Save your sticker (Save to Photos on iPhone, Download picture elsewhere), then open your profile settings in the app and choose the saved image as your photo. The design stays inside the circle that profile photos are cropped to.
When should I switch to I voted?
After you vote. Under What should it say?, choose I voted, or I voted early or I voted by mail if that fits.
Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
