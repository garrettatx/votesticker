## Facts

- Source: site/states.js. Status on October 20: In-person OCT 22–29; upcoming.
- Early voting runs OCT 22–29. If you run this on or after OCT 22, the date line is BY OCT 29 instead. If you run this after OCT 29, early voting is over: use VOTE / NOV 3 with ELECTION DAY as the main message and keep only I VOTED / EARLY from the early sayings.
- Capital: Annapolis, on the west shore of the Chesapeake Bay. Star at 68% across and 41% down the outline's bounding box (site/state-shapes.js).
- Outline: Keep the long western panhandle and the bay shape accurate; Maryland is wide and thin, so keep the outline small above the headline
- State palette: background #F6F1E7, ink #1C1C1C, accent #C98A00. Core 4 reverses it: #1C1C1C background.
- Ready-made images on the site now: 6. This set adds ten; Claude decides what stays.
