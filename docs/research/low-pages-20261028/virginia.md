Mail-ballot errors: one real precision correction

Your copy says:

“If your ballot reaches the registrar by the Friday before Election Day, the registrar will tell you by mail or email within three days…”

The statute says notification must occur within three days of finding the problem—not necessarily within three days of receiving the ballot. For this election, the applicable cure process covers ballots received by Friday, October 30, with corrections due before noon November 6.
