Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your county board of elections, starting the day after registration closed. On Election Day, vote at your assigned polling place between 6:30 a.m. and 7:30 p.m.
Early voting is at your county board of elections. On Election Day, go to the polling place for your precinct. At the wrong polling place, you'll be asked to vote a provisional ballot.
Check your registration and polling location online before you go. When you vote in person, the only thing you need to bring is an accepted photo ID.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Ohio driver's license
State of Ohio ID card
Interim ID form from the Ohio BMV
U.S. passport or passport card
U.S. military ID or military dependent ID
Ohio National Guard ID
U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs ID
Your photo ID can't be expired. A driver's license or ID from another state doesn't count, and neither does the Ohio Mobile ID.
No photo ID? You vote a provisional ballot. For it to count, show an accepted ID at your county board of elections within four days after Election Day.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any registered Ohio voter can vote absentee, no reason needed. You have to ask for a ballot, with a separate application for each election.
Sign the identification envelope and give one form of ID: your Ohio driver's license or state ID number, the last four digits of your Social Security number, or a copy of your photo ID.
Bring it to your county board of elections, or use the secure drop box most boards have, by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day. The drop box is open 24/7 until polls close.
If your identification envelope is incomplete or doesn't match your record, the board mails you a notice. Get the missing information to the board on its form, in person or by mail, by the fourth day after the election.

Ohio and the I Voted Sticker
In 2019 the Secretary of State ran a contest for students in grades 6 to 12 to design Ohio's I Voted sticker. Over 2,000 entries came in, and more than 15,000 votes picked the winner: Emily Legg, a 12th grader at Teays Valley High School in Pickaway County.
Do I get an I voted sticker if I vote early in Ohio?
Yes, while supplies last. The Secretary of State sent every county board of elections a limited supply of America 250 "I Voted" stickers for voters who vote in person, early or on Election Day.

When is the mail ballot deadline in Ohio?

From the Ohio Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Ohio, and does it count?
You may get one if your name isn't in the pollbook, you don't have a valid photo ID, you already asked for an absentee ballot, or you moved without updating your registration. It counts once officials confirm you're eligible. Without ID, bring one to your county board of elections within four days.

What if I am in line when the polls close in Ohio?
You can still vote. Anyone in line by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day has the right to vote.

How do I track my mail ballot in Ohio?
Your absentee ballot comes with a tracking number. Track it through your county board of elections or the state's tracking system.

Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
Do you keep my information?
Your sticker is made in your browser and never uploaded, and the site never asks for a photo, a name or an email address. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics count visits; the About page lists exactly what they record.
