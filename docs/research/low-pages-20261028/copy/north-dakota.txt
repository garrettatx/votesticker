When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
In North Dakota, each county decides whether to offer early voting. For this election, Burleigh, Cass, Grand Forks, Morton, Stark, Stutsman and Ward counties do. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
A county can open early voting up to 15 days before Election Day. The Secretary of State's precinct page lists each county's dates, sites and hours. Bring a valid ID.
Election Day hours: Hours vary by county or city. Polls open between 7 a.m. and 9 a.m. and close between 7 p.m. and 9 p.m. local time. North Dakota has two time zones.
ID: Yes. Show a North Dakota driver's license, nondriver ID card, or tribal ID with your name, current North Dakota address, and birth date. A utility bill or bank statement can fill in missing details.

Where You Can Vote
Some counties offer early voting on set dates and times before Election Day; check whether yours does. On Election Day, vote at the polling location for your precinct, or at any vote center in your county if your county uses them. Either way, a poll worker checks your ID and asks if you're 18, a U.S. citizen and have lived in North Dakota for the last 30 days.
Enter your address in the My Voting Information portal to see your polling location, its hours and your sample ballot. Some counties use vote centers, where you can vote at any one in the county.
Moved since your ND ID was issued? Update it with the Department of Transportation if you can. If not, bring your ID plus a current bank statement, utility bill, paycheck or government document with your new address.

What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
North Dakota driver's license
North Dakota non-driver ID
Tribal ID or tribal letter
Long-term care certificate from a North Dakota facility you live in
No valid ID with you? You can vote a set-aside ballot. It counts once you show your ID to your county auditor, in person or electronically, within 13 days after the election, before the County Canvassing Board meets.

Who Can Vote by Mail
Any eligible voter can vote absentee, but you have to ask first: no one is mailed a ballot without an application. In vote-by-mail counties, the county auditor mails you the application.
Sign the return envelope yourself. Your county compares that signature with the one on your application.
Every county has secure absentee ballot drop boxes. Get your ballot in one by the close of polls on Election Day, or hand it to your county auditor.
If your signature is missing or doesn't match, your county auditor will contact you. Confirm it's yours in person with your ID, or by letter or email with a copy of the ID you used to apply, before the county canvassing board meets on Monday, November 16.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign buttons and hats at home. Within 100 feet of the polling place entrance, and inside, you can't wear political badges, buttons or insignias, or try to sway anyone's vote.
If you're in line when the polls close, stay in line. You can still vote. People who arrive after closing can't.

When are polls open in North Dakota on Election Day?
Hours vary by county or city. Polls open between 7 a.m. and 9 a.m. and close between 7 p.m. and 9 p.m. local time. North Dakota has two time zones.

Do I need ID to vote in North Dakota?
Yes. Show a North Dakota driver's license, nondriver ID card, or tribal ID with your name, current North Dakota address, and birth date. A utility bill or bank statement can fill in missing details.

Can I still register to vote in North Dakota?
You don't need to. North Dakota has no voter registration. If you're eligible, bring a valid ID with your current address and vote.
From the North Dakota Secretary of State .
When is the mail ballot deadline in North Dakota?
Your ballot must be received by the close of polls on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Mail it with your own postage, use a county drop box, or hand it to your county auditor. Polling places won't take it.
From the North Dakota Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in North Dakota, and does it count?
North Dakota uses a set-aside ballot if you don't have a valid ID with you. Show your ID to the county auditor within 13 days after the election, before the County Canvassing Board meets, and your ballot will be counted.
From the North Dakota Secretary of State .
How do I check that my mail ballot was counted in North Dakota?
Use the state's absentee ballot tracker to see whether your county received your ballot.

Is it too late to mail my ballot in North Dakota?
Don't bring it to your polling place; absentee ballots aren't accepted there on Election Day. Put it in a county drop box or hand it to your county auditor before polls close.

Is this an official election site?
No. Vote Sticker is an independent, nonpartisan tool made by one person. For registration, polling places, and deadlines, go to your state or local election office. Each state page links to it.
Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
