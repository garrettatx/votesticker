When, Where, and What to Bring
Vote Early
Early voting in Tennessee runs Wednesday, October 14 to Thursday, October 29. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Each county sets its early voting sites and hours. Check your county election commission.
Election Day hours: Opening times vary by county. All polls close at 8 p.m. Eastern or 7 p.m. Central time.
ID: Yes. Show a federal or Tennessee photo ID, such as a Tennessee driver's license, a U.S. passport, or a military ID.

Where You Can Vote
Vote early at your county election commission office or a satellite site it opens. No reason needed. On Election Day, vote at your assigned polling place.
The state's voter lookup shows your early voting and Election Day information, your polling place, and your absentee ballot status. The free GoVoteTN app has polling places, hours and sample ballots too.
Moved since you registered? Vote early. You can change the address on your registration and vote at the same early voting site.
From the Tennessee Secretary of State . Last checked October 25, 2026.
What ID to Bring
Bring one of these:
Tennessee driver's license with your photo
U.S. passport
Photo ID from the Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security
Photo ID from the federal or Tennessee state government
U.S. military photo ID
Tennessee handgun carry permit with your photo
An expired ID still works. First-time voter who registered by mail or online? With an expired ID, also bring a current utility bill, bank statement, government check, paycheck or other government document with your name and address. College IDs and IDs from other states don't count.
Forgot your photo ID? You vote a provisional ballot. Then you have two business days after Election Day to show a valid photo ID at your county election commission office.

Who Can Vote by Mail
You can vote absentee by mail in Tennessee if you:
are 60 or older
will be outside your county during all of early voting and all day on Election Day
are hospitalized, ill or physically disabled and can't get to your polling place, or care for someone who is
or your spouse are a full-time student at a college outside your county
live in a nursing home, assisted living facility or home for the aged outside your county
are a candidate in the election
observe a religious holiday that keeps you from voting in person during early voting and on Election Day
serve as an Election Day official or work for the election commission
can't vote in person because of jury duty
have a disability and an inaccessible polling place
or your spouse hold a CDL or TWIC card and will be working out of the state or county during early voting and Election Day, with no address to get mail there
are in the military or a U.S. citizen living overseas
are on the permanent absentee list
Tennessee has no drop-off for mail ballots. Send yours by mail or a carrier like FedEx or UPS. You can't hand it to a poll worker during early voting or on Election Day.

Phones, Photos, and Campaign Gear
Leave the campaign shirts and gear at home. Voters wearing campaign clothing or paraphernalia aren't allowed within 100 feet of a polling place entrance, where campaign materials and asking for votes are banned.
All polls close at 8 p.m. Eastern or 7 p.m. Central. If you're in line when polls close, stay put. You'll be allowed to vote.

When are polls open in Tennessee on Election Day?

Can I still register to vote in Tennessee?
No, not for the November 3 election. The deadline was October 5, 2026, and Tennessee has no Election Day registration.

Can I take a picture of my ballot in Tennessee?
Not at the polls. Tennessee law lets you use your phone in the polling place for voting information, but not to take photos or video.

When is the mail ballot deadline in Tennessee?
Mail it in time for your county election commission to get it by the close of polls on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. It has to go by mail or a carrier like FedEx or UPS. You can't hand it in.
From the Tennessee Secretary of State .
What is a provisional ballot in Tennessee, and does it count?
If you don't bring a photo ID, you vote a provisional ballot. To have it reviewed, show a valid photo ID at your county election commission office within two business days after Election Day and sign an affidavit there.
From the Tennessee Secretary of State .
What if I am in line when the polls close in Tennessee?
You can still vote. If you're in line when polls close at 8 p.m. Eastern or 7 p.m. Central, you'll be allowed to vote.

How do I track my mail ballot in Tennessee?
Use the state's Voter Registration Information Lookup. It tracks absentee ballots along with your registration and polling place.

Where do the dates come from?
From official state sources: the election office's own pages or state law, checked and dated. Until a state's dates are confirmed, its stickers say Election Day.
Why do some states say hours or start dates vary?
In some states, counties or towns set their own early voting days, hours, and locations. Your local election office has the final details.
What if my state doesn't have early voting?
Your sticker says VOTE NOV 3. If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you may qualify for an absentee ballot. Your state's page links to the rules.
